#Privacy choices worth knowing

OptionDefaultEffect
Send typing indicatoronOthers see "typing…" while you write
Animate Tenor and Giphy GIFsoffDownloads from their site, which sees your IP. When off, you get a still preview through Discord
Streamer modeoffHides your email and username in settings
Message logoffDeleted messages stay visible, marked, and edits keep the original. Local only
Keep messages on diskonChats open instantly and Ctrl+F works offline. Only your user can read the files

#Files on disk

$XDG_CONFIG_HOME and $XDG_CACHE_HOME replace ~/.config and ~/.cache when set.

PathWhat it is
~/.config/sigmacord/settings.kdlSettings, including per-theme options and colors
~/.config/sigmacord/themes/*.kdlYour themes
~/.config/sigmacord/plugins/*.luaYour plugins
~/.config/sigmacord/plugins.kdlWhich plugins are off, the hosts you approved, and the side panel
~/.config/sigmacord/plugin-data/What each plugin stores, per account
~/.config/sigmacord/drafts.kdlUnsent drafts per channel (chmod 600)
~/.config/sigmacord/saved-<account>.jsonSaved messages and notes
~/.config/sigmacord/accountsSaved accounts: ID, name and avatar only
~/.config/sigmacord/tokens/<ID>Remembered tokens when there's no keyring (chmod 600)
~/.config/sigmacord/tokenOptional token file (chmod 600)
~/.cache/sigmacord/messagesMessage cache: folders 700, files 600, 200 messages per channel, 50 MB cap
~/.cache/wal/colorsRead only: pywal's palette, for themes that use it

To go back to defaults, close the app and delete ~/.config/sigmacord/settings.kdl.
